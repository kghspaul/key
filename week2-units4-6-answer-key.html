Q1 看圖辨義｜U5

English

W: Is your arm bothering you?
M: Yeah, I think I strained it.
W: Have you seen a doctor yet?
M: No, I haven’t had time yet.
W: You should see one today.
Q: Which picture shows the man’s situation?

中文

女：你的手臂不舒服嗎？
男：對，我想我拉傷了。
女：你已經看過醫生了嗎？
男：還沒有，我還沒時間。
女：你今天應該去看醫生。
問：哪一張圖片呈現了這名男子的情況？

解析：strain 作動詞可表示「拉傷（肌肉／身體部位）」。因此要找的是男子手臂受傷、正在不舒服的畫面；原卷正解為 (A)。其他圖片沒有同時呈現「手臂拉傷」這個核心情境。
答案：(A)

Q2 看圖辨義｜U6

English

Which two of the following are true based on the chart?
(A) Eighteen percent of architecture firms are falling short on many goals.
(B) Almost half of all architecture firms are meeting most goals.
(C) Only twenty percent of architecture firms are exceeding goals.
(D) Less than ten percent of architecture firms are meeting all goals at all.

中文

根據圖表，下列哪兩項敘述正確？
(A) 18% 的建築公司在許多目標上未達標。
(B) 幾乎一半的建築公司達成大多數目標。
(C) 只有 20% 的建築公司超越目標。
(D) 不到 10% 的建築公司達成所有目標。

讀圖：圖表數據為 Falling short 13%、Meeting all 18%、Exceeding 20%、Meeting most 49%。所以 (B) 49% 幾乎一半，正確；(C) 20% 正確。
誤答檢驗：(A) 把 13% 誤看成 18%；(D) 把 18% 誤說成不到 10%。
答案：(B), (C)

R1 詞彙

English

Unable to stand the tense ____ in the classroom before the exam, Billy had to leave the classroom to calm down for a bit.

中文

考試前教室裡的緊張氣氛讓 Billy 無法忍受，只好離開教室冷靜一下。

(A) gallery
(B) formula
(C) atmosphere
(D) contribution
詳解：atmosphere = 氣氛；tense atmosphere = 緊張的氣氛。gallery（畫廊）、formula（公式）、contribution（貢獻）都不符合「考前教室的緊張氛圍」。
答案：(C)

R2 詞彙

English

It takes the Earth around 365.24 days to fully ____ the Sun, which is longer than an ordinary year. As a result, a leap year was introduced.

中文

地球繞太陽一周約需 365.24 天，比一般的一年稍長，因此有了閏年的制度。

(A) orbit
(B) forge
(C) strain
(D) loosen

R6–R12 文意選填｜夢境與科學

English

Do dreams have anything to do with science? Usually, the workshop of science is the laboratory. However, some important scientific breakthroughs didn’t occur in labs. They happened in the world of dreams. For centuries, scientists have described moments when solutions appeared through images and patterns seen in sleep, rather than through careful calculations. While this may sound mysterious, modern neuroscience suggests that the brain continues to process information and form connections even when we’re not consciously thinking. One of the most famous examples involves Dmitri Mendeleev. In 1869, after struggling to organize the chemical elements, he fell asleep. In his dream, he saw a table where all the elements fell into place. Upon waking, he wrote down what we now recognize as the periodic table. Similarly, August Kekulé could not determine the structure of benzene for a long time. Then he dreamed of a snake biting its own tail, forming a ring. The striking vision led him to propose a circular molecular structure. As it turned out, it was a key finding in organic chemistry.

中文

夢境和科學有關嗎？通常，科學研究的「工作坊」就是實驗室。然而，一些重要的科學突破並不是在實驗室裡發生的，而是出現在夢境的世界中。幾世紀以來，科學家一直描述一些時刻：解答是透過睡眠中看見的圖像與模式浮現，而不是透過仔細的計算。雖然這聽起來很神秘，但現代神經科學指出，即使我們沒有有意識地思考，大腦仍會持續處理資訊並建立連結。 其中一個最著名的例子涉及 Dmitri Mendeleev。1869 年，他苦苦思索如何組織化學元素後睡著了。他夢見一張表格，所有元素都落在適當的位置。醒來後，他把它寫下來——也就是我們現在所認識的元素週期表。同樣地，August Kekulé 長期無法確定苯的結構。後來某晚，他夢見一條蛇咬住自己的尾巴，形成一個環。這個引人注目的景象使他提出環狀分子結構；事後證明，這是有機化學中的重要發現。

答案：6. (E) breakthroughs　7. (G) calculations　8. (B) process　9. (F) organize　10. (C) recognize　11. (D) determine　12. (A) vision
(A) vision
(B) process
(C) recognize
(D) determine
(E) breakthroughs
(F) organize
(G) calculations
逐題詳解：
第 6 題：breakthroughs
scientific breakthroughs = 科學突破；複數名詞符合 some important scientific ___。
第 7 題：calculations
careful calculations = 仔細計算；與前面的 solutions appeared through images and patterns 對比。
第 8 題：process
process information = 處理資訊，是 neuroscience 常見搭配；brain continues to process information。
第 9 題：organize
struggle to + V = 努力／苦於做某事；organize the chemical elements = 組織化學元素。
第 10 題：recognize
what we now recognize as... = 我們現在所認為／辨認為……；as 引出身分或角色。
第 11 題：determine
determine the structure = 確定結構；could not + 原形動詞。
第 12 題：vision
the striking vision = 引人注目的景象；前文的 snake dream 是一個 vision。
答案：6(E)／7(G)／8(B)／9(F)／10(C)／11(D)／12(A)

R13–R15 閱讀測驗｜Jianzi

English

Jianzi is an imaginative sport that originated in China and has been enjoyed for centuries. This traditional game involves keeping a small weighted shuttlecock in the air by using the feet, knees, chest, shoulders, and other parts of the body, except for the hands. Historians believe that Jianzi developed from an ancient Chinese activity called cuju, a ball game that was popular more than 2,000 years ago. Over time, people began using a feathered shuttlecock instead of a ball, creating a game that was easier to play in small spaces and required excellent balance and coordination. There are several variations of Jianzi competitions. In the most common version, a player or group of players tries to keep the shuttlecock from touching the ground for as long as possible. Another popular form is professional competitive Jianzi, in which teams compete on opposite sides of a net, similar to volleyball or badminton. Players use their feet and bodies to send the shuttlecock over the net while attempting to prevent the opposing team from returning it. There are also freestyle competitions that reward creativity, difficult tricks, and precise control. Although Jianzi originated in China, it has spread to many other countries. In Vietnam, a closely related game called đá cầu (dah kow) is widely played. Similar shuttlecock-kicking games can also be found in South Korea, Germany, Hungary, and several other nations. International tournaments have helped increase the sport’s popularity and introduced it to new audiences. Today, Jianzi is a popular game played in parks, schools, and sports clubs around the world.

中文

毽子是一項源自中國、流傳數百年的富創意運動。這項傳統遊戲要用腳、膝蓋、胸部、肩膀及身體其他部位（手除外）讓一個小型加重毽子保持在空中。歷史學家認為，毽子發展自古代中國的一項活動「蹴鞠」；蹴鞠是一種兩千多年前相當流行的球類遊戲。隨著時間推移，人們開始以帶羽毛的毽子取代球，形成一種更適合在小空間進行、並需要良好平衡與協調能力的遊戲。 毽子比賽有多種形式。最常見的版本中，一名玩家或一組玩家會盡可能長時間讓毽子不落地。另一種受歡迎的形式是職業競技毽子，兩隊在球網兩側比賽，形式類似排球或羽球。玩家用腳與身體將毽子踢過網，同時試圖阻止對方回擊。另有自由式比賽，會獎勵創意、高難度技巧與精準控制。 雖然毽子起源於中國，但如今已傳播到許多其他國家。在越南，一種密切相關的遊戲 đá cầu（dah kow）十分普遍。南韓、德國、匈牙利及其他國家也能看到類似的踢毽遊戲。國際賽事幫助提升這項運動的知名度，並讓新的觀眾認識它。如今，毽子在世界各地的公園、學校與運動俱樂部都很常見。
